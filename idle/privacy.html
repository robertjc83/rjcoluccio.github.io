Strata Tycoon Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Summary

Strata Tycoon does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information.

What the game stores

Your game progress (credits, depth, upgrades and similar game values) is saved only on your own device, in the app's local storage. It never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in or contact details
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising identifiers
  • No ads and no in-app purchases
  • No access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files

Children

Because the game collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If the game ever adds features that handle personal data (such as ads, purchases or cloud saves), this page will be updated before that version is released.
