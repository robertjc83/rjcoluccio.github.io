Hollow Pines

Keep the Hearth burning. They come when the light dies.

How to play
  • Day: chop trees, mine stone and iron, pick berries. Build walls, gates, spikes, torches and towers inside the Hearth's glow.
  • Night: Hollowkin pour out of the forest. They go for you, or for the Hearth. If the Hearth goes out, the run is over.
  • Workbench: stand next to it to upgrade your axe, pickaxe, weapon and lantern. Level it up to unlock iron and Hollowbone tiers.
  • Light burns them. Torches and the Hearth damage creatures inside their glow.
  • Level up from gathering, building and kills. Each level lets you pick a perk.
