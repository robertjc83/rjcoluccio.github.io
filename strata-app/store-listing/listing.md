## Full description (max 4000)

```
Dig deeper. Get richer.

Strata Tycoon is an idle mining game. Build a drilling operation, bore straight down through an alien planet, and turn every layer of rock into credits, even while you're away.

⛏ DRILL THROUGH THE LAYERS
Topsoil, granite, iron veins, gold reefs, crystal caves, magma, all the way to the core. Every layer is tougher to dig, but its ore is worth far more.

🚚 RUN THE OPERATION
Your rigs dig ore into the silo and your haulers sell it. If the silo fills up, the drill stalls, so keep digging, storage and hauling in balance to maximise profit.

⚡ OVERDRIVE
Hold the button to push your drill past its limits for a burst of speed. Tap glittering geodes for bonus credits.

🚀 LAUNCH TO NEW WORLDS
Hit 500 m to unlock a launch. Leave your planet behind for Core Shards, a permanent boost to every run. Each new planet is harder rock with richer ore.

🌙 EARNS WHILE YOU'RE AWAY
Your crew keeps working the night shift. Come back to a pile of credits.

✔ Retro pixel style
✔ Plays offline
✔ No ads, no in-app purchases, no account
✔ No data collected
```
