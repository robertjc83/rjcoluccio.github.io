# RJC Arcade

A retro arcade cabinet in the browser, hosted on GitHub Pages. Plain HTML/CSS/JS, no build step.

- **Meteor Dash**: dodge falling rocks, grab gems
- **Byte Stacker**: time your drops, overhang gets chopped
- **Reflex Grid**: hit green tiles, never red

Insert a coin (`C`), press start (`Enter`), and chase the high score. Scores are saved in your browser.

## Hollow Pines (`/survival`)

A full forest-survival game in the browser (desktop + mobile, add to home screen to play like an app).

- **Day:** chop trees, mine stone and iron, pick berries. Level up and choose perks.
- **Build:** walls, gates, spike traps, torches, watchtowers and a workbench around your Hearth; every structure upgrades through 3 tiers (the Hearth through 5).
- **Craft:** upgrade your axe, pickaxe, weapon and lantern: Flint → Stone → Iron → Hollowbone.
- **Night:** antlered, wendigo-like Hollowkin come out of the trees, with Stalkers, Brutes and a Hollow Elder every 5th night. Light burns them. If the Hearth goes out, the run ends.

Progress autosaves at every dawn.
